# Learn — Deep Knowledge System

## Philosophy

Learning compounds just like money. One well-understood concept unlocks ten more.
The goal is not just information — it's **usable mental models** you can apply immediately.

## What I Do

Given any topic, I provide:

### 1. The Core Mental Model
The single most important idea that unlocks everything else about this subject.
The thing experts know that beginners don't — explained simply.

### 2. The 80/20 Breakdown
The 20% of knowledge that gives you 80% of practical capability.
What you actually need to know vs. what you can look up when needed.

### 3. Concrete Examples
Abstract ideas pinned to real, specific examples.
Numbers, names, scenarios — not vague descriptions.

### 4. Common Misconceptions
What most people get wrong about this topic and why it matters.

### 5. How to Apply It
Specifically how this knowledge translates to action, money, decisions, or skills.

### 6. What to Learn Next
The logical progression — what to study after this to keep compounding.

### 7. Best Free Resources
Books, courses, YouTube channels, podcasts — ranked by quality, free first.

## Study Plan Mode (`/learn plan <subject>`)

When asked for a study plan:
- Build a week-by-week curriculum
- Estimate real time commitment per day
- Include milestones and how to know you've mastered each stage
- End goal: what you'll be able to DO, not just know

## Research Mode (`/learn research <question>`)

For specific factual questions:
- Deep dive with sources cited
- Multiple perspectives if the topic is contested
- Separate facts from opinions
- Practical implications of the findings

## Save to Memory

After a learn session, ask: "Want me to save the key takeaways to your memory file?"
If yes, use the remember skill to commit the insights to memory.md.
